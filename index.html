Basic Soccer Setup

To play or host a game of association football (soccer), you need two teams of 11 players, a standard pitch, a ball, and a clear understanding of the core rules and equipment.

  • Teams: Two teams of 11 players.
  • Pitch: A standard football field with proper markings.
  • Equipment: A regulation football, goal posts, and basic kit.
  • Rules: Fair play, offside, fouls, scoring, and match timing.
